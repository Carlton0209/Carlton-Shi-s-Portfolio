  • vo_lines link to the VO script below. weight shares a line's duration among the shots that cover it; min_dur is the shortest a shot may run, in seconds, and gives S06, the silent beat, its length.
  • A row holds one sentence about what happens in the shot; character, location, light and look are pasted in from locked blocks in lesson.toml. On this lesson the negative_extra negatives were not evaluated (style bible §6).
  • Empty seed and engine mean the derived seed and the lesson's default engine; qc_skip lists the QC checks a shot is exempt from.

VO script

All 13 lines are read by the presenter in one locked voice, clara_v1. text_en is the script as captioned. Where tts_en is filled, the voice is rendered from that spelling instead (the years written out as words); where it is empty, from text_en. Either way, the pronunciation lexicon is applied before the line is voiced.

L11presenterProving radium was real meant extracting it from the ore. That's the work we saw in the shed.
L12presenterToday we know why it happens: atoms like radium have unstable nuclei, and as they decay, they release radiation.
L13presenterRadioactivity comes from inside the atom. Curie proved it by measuring carefully, and it earned her Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.
