The Glow in the Shed

An 88-second, instructor-led lesson on radioactivity, told through one night in Marie Curie's lab.

Course Chemistry / Physical Science, grades 9-12
Standard NGSS HS-PS1-8 (nuclear processes and radioactive decay)
Runtime 87.9 s, timed from the rendered VO
Language English
Shots 21: 7 instructor shots (lip-synced, two camera setups), 11 B-roll, 3 full-frame graphics, plus 7 graphics cues over picture
Instructor on camera 45 s of the 88

Learning objectives

After watching, a student can:

  1. Say that radioactivity comes from inside the atom, not from a chemical reaction.
  2. Describe the evidence: ray strength depended only on the amount of uranium.
  3. Explain why pitchblende's extra activity pointed to new elements (polonium and radium).

Structure

Time Shots Beat
0:00-0:18 S01-S07 Cold open, 1902. A leaky shed in Paris, under the instructor's voice. Curie turns down the lamp and stands among her vials in the dark (the faint blue glow the shot list asks for does not show in the delivered S06 and S07). No explanation yet.
0:18-0:21 S08 Title card over the held last frame of S07.
0:21-0:52 S09-S14 The instructor takes over. Becquerel's rays (on camera, lower third), Curie's electrometer (insert + diagram), the conclusion that the rays come from the atoms themselves (on camera, cutting A/B/A as graphics build on the wall beside her).
0:52-1:09 S15-S17 Two new elements, the tiles landing on the words, then back to the courtyard: extracting radium is the work we saw in the shed.
1:09-1:17 S18-S19 Today we know why. She hands over to the diagram mid-sentence; it is labelled as modern understanding.
1:17-1:28 S20-S21 The recap on the close-up, and the end card over the glowing shelf.

The cold open is set in 1902, at the payoff, and the explainer flashes back to 1896-1898. Students see the wonder first and get the reasoning second; "that's the work we saw in the shed" closes the loop.

Accuracy

Every on-screen fact comes from this table. If a fact isn't here, it doesn't go in the film. Open each link once more before the final export: a fact-check that was right when written is only as good as its last read. Curie, ch. V is her own account: chapter V of Marie Curie's Pierre Curie (1923, translated by Charlotte and Vernon Kellogg). It is cited beside every AIP source, because on 2026-11-16 the history.aip.org pages showed a maintenance notice.

One figure stayed out of the script: Curie's own account gives batches of up to twenty kilograms (Curie, ch. V); the line says "tons of ore", which the same chapter supports (several tons of residue).

What went wrong in production, and what the pipeline does about it

Problem (as it happened) Fix
The lexicon written for ElevenLabs made Qwen3-TTS worse: "ray-dee-um" came back as "Hooray DM"; 8 of 13 lines failed the Whisper check Aliases kept only for the two words this voice gets wrong (Henri, Becquerel), other terms phoneme-only; all 13 lines pass. The check now maps respellings and split compounds back before scoring
Krea-2 kept giving Marie eye contact with the lens Gaze written as an action, S03 reframed as a side profile, three candidates, producer picks pinned
The "lamp turned down" shots came out fully lit The dark shed got its own look; CFG-1 models ignore negative prompts, so darkness is written as light
The blackboard filled with garbled chalk writing in every keyframe Left out of the set description (the real shed had one: Curie, ch. V). Krea-2 still painted a board with faint, unreadable chalk marks into four of the five S02 and S05 candidates, and the two picked (S02 v1, S05 v2) shipped it, at 3.92-5.83 s and 9.67-11.92 s; the one board-free candidate, S02 v3, has her looking into the lens. OCR was not installed, so the text check skipped every take, and the pick by eye missed the board
H3 crashed at 1280x720 H3 sizes are multiples of 32: 1280x736, enforced by validate
A 2-second insert costs a 5.2-second H3 render Accepted; the planner shows it, and the engine registry can route inserts elsewhere
H3's rain under the VO was inaudible (-44 LUFS) Every ambience bed is measured and lifted to -30 LUFS in conform (by at most 24 dB), then ducked under the VO in the mix
The lamp-dimming shot needed a lighting change mid-shot H3 handled it in one image-to-video take (S05)
S09's first presenter take matched the voice only weakly (lip-sync confidence 0.53) The gate rejected it; a new seed passed (0.76). A three-way experiment showed no recipe change helps more than a new sample (see the models doc)
S15, the 12-second take, matched clearly (0.93) but its mouth ran 3 frames ahead of the voice Conform slips the picture 3 frames instead of re-rendering a 53-minute take; re-measured on the delivered master: +0.04 frames
S10's take froze for its last two frames inside the cut The gate judges only the span the edit uses; this one was inside it, so S10 was regenerated
Re-made in the editor graph, S18 failed the in-graph lip-sync score (+2.6 frames) although the batch QC had passed the delivered S18 (+1.2 frames, 52 ms) The node was scoring the whole take, including the 2.25 s that H3's 124-frame minimum adds past the cut, where the match with the voice drifts later and turns ambiguous; measured both ways, the editor and batch takes read the same. The node now judges the span that ships, with the lesson's thresholds and the batch QC's verdict function: +1.3 frames on the re-made take, pass
The delivered S18 differs slightly from a fresh render of the same shot (37.7 dB PSNR), although four renders of S18 on 2026-11-07 (three from the editor graph, one batch) were bit-identical The VO was re-leveled after the take was rendered, and after five other presenter takes too, and nothing noticed. QC now compares the input hashes every take carries with today's files on each run, and compares a changed voice as speech: all six carry the same speech at the same times (within 0.85 dB once the level change is taken out), so they ship with a warning. Kept as delivered: a new render would make them exact, for 1.7 GPU hours and a rebuilt master
In S06, the silent lamp-down beat (11.92-15.92 s), the glass roof brightens into daylight and green leaves from about 12.7 s, after the lamp has gone down Not caught: no QC check looks at what a shot shows, and S06 is exempt from the black and freeze checks by design. Shipped as delivered; a matte over the roof in conform that leaves her head alone, or a new S06, would fix it
The title card (S08, 18.42-21.42 s) plays over 3 s of room tone: only the B-roll shots get an ambience bed, so the S07 bed stops at the cut Not caught: delivery QC measures the loudness of the whole master, not gaps in the beds. Shipped as delivered; carrying the unused tail of the S07 take's sound under the title, faded out, is a re-finish on the CPU
Two graphics beats land at their cuts: G03's current beat (33.54 s) comes 6 frames before the cut at 33.79 s, so the needle, which moves 10 frames after it, never moves; G04's "2× uranium" beat is on screen for 8 frames before the cut at 41.46 s Not caught: mograph reports a beat that falls outside its cue, and both fall inside. Shipped as delivered; earlier word anchors in graphics.csv and a re-render of G03 and G04 would fix it, on the CPU
The runner stopped twice mid-render (S06, S13) while ComfyUI went on rendering Both takes were fetched from ComfyUI's history by out/smoke/fetch_take.py, which appends the manifest record (marked recovered, with the server's execution time). The runner cannot re-attach to a job in flight, so resuming would have rendered both again (7.3 and 8.8 GPU minutes). Next: store prompt ids and re-attach on resume

Production steps (the commands that made it)

python -m lessonforge validate projects/curie_radium --server http://127.0.0.1:8188
python -m lessonforge tts projects/curie_radium --design      # lock the voice once
python -m lessonforge tts projects/curie_radium               # every line, lexicon applied
python -m lessonforge tts projects/curie_radium --check       # Whisper: WER and key terms
python -m lessonforge plan projects/curie_radium              # the VO is now the clock
python -m lessonforge setups projects/curie_radium            # setup A candidates
python -m lessonforge setups projects/curie_radium --approve A:1
python -m lessonforge setups projects/curie_radium --derive B --candidates 3
python -m lessonforge setups projects/curie_radium --approve B:1
python -m lessonforge run projects/curie_radium --stage keyframe
python -m lessonforge pin projects/curie_radium --stage keyframe S01:1 S02:1 S03:3 S04:3 S05:2 S06:2 S07:2 S10:3 S16:3 S17:2 S21:2
python -m lessonforge run projects/curie_radium --stage video
python -m lessonforge qc projects/curie_radium --stage video     # S10's first take froze inside the cut
python -m lessonforge run projects/curie_radium --stage video --regen
python -m lessonforge qc projects/curie_radium --stage video
python -m lessonforge run projects/curie_radium --stage avatar --timeout 4000   # S15's 12-second take ran 53.6 min
python -m lessonforge qc projects/curie_radium --stage avatar    # lip sync, and inputs against today's files
python -m lessonforge run projects/curie_radium --stage avatar --regen          # S09's first take failed lip sync
python -m lessonforge qc projects/curie_radium --stage avatar
python -m lessonforge syncqc projects/curie_radium               # lip-sync plots for every presenter take
(cd mograph && npm ci)                                             # once: Remotion and the fonts
python -m lessonforge mograph projects/curie_radium
python -m lessonforge conform projects/curie_radium
python -m lessonforge finish projects/curie_radium
python -m lessonforge report projects/curie_radium

This is the order to run them in. Of the eleven keyframe pins, S01:1 and S02:1 are the two that change the result: without a pin, and with no keyframe QC run, the newest candidate is used. The default job timeout is 1800 s, so run --stage avatar needs a longer one for S15. Eight lines were rendered again after the lexicon fix (take 2 of L02, L04, L06 and L09-L13 in vo/en/manifest.json).

Two things went differently on this lesson. Setup A's four candidates are renders from a smoke test (Krea-2, from the hand-written prompt in out/smoke/presenter_acam.txt), copied into out/curie_radium/setup/ and approved with --approve A:1; only setup B was made by setups (--derive B). And the keyframes and the first six B-roll takes (S01-S06) were rendered before the VO existed, with shot lengths estimated from word counts; all six sit at H3's 124-frame floor either way, so the real VO did not change their frame counts.
